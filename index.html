THE METHOD

A Precision Framework for Complex Builds.

We minimize construction anomalies through a highly structured 4-step workflow that anchors budget alignments and regulatory clearances before hammers hit surfaces.

Book consultation arrow_forward
01

Site Consultation & Feasibility

We conduct on-site physical walk-throughs to inspect joists, framing structures, structural pathways, plumbing conditions, and analyze co-op alteration logs.

02

Pre-Construction & Board Filings

Compiling design documents for landmarks commissions (LPC), coordinating engineering approvals, drawing lines of utility layouts, and submitting board approval binders.

03

Architectural Construction

Direct project management including coordinate logistics, MEP installations, custom millwork fittings, marble setting, and strict dust protections.

04

White-Glove Handover

Reviewing rigorous checklist items, performing white-glove site cleaning, DOB sign-off closures, and transferring keys to client legacy.
